What people said about this work/theme?

Strengths

"I would contend that, whether people are conscious of it or not, ethics is always involved in robotic design and use. Every choice in design or application is an ethical decision. What kind of materials should you use? (eg. are they sustainable? who manufactured them? does the texture suggest human-like sentience?). What you're doing is making these ethical decisions conscious, so that they can be more intentional."
dr. Madelaine Ley Robotics Philosopher
"I think it’s overly optimistic to assume that technology can reliably be ethics driven. I think technology is mostly economics driven, but if we can incentivize ethics through an economic system maybe it would be more plausible. Although that is difficult to envision."

Weaknesses

"I've raised concerns regarding how sex robots afford a type of masculine (idiotic) sexual enjoyment, which disavows and devalues other (non-phallic) modes of sexual enjoyment. As (desirable) sexuality is constructed and embedded in patriarchal and heteronormative socio-political orders - and normative sex robot design can naturalise these orders - it is key to imagine and design sex robots in less phallic ways. I have offered some suggestions to do so through a queer Lacanian lens on (technological) sexual enjoyment."
mv. Maaike van der Horst Techno-Psychoanalyst
